# Jhin TFT carry item build guide

Champion: Jhin. Role: attack-damage backline carry. Core item recommendation: Infinity Edge (Vô Cực
Kiếm), Deathblade (Kiếm Tử Thần), and Giant Slayer (Diệt Khổng Lồ). Strong alternatives include
Last Whisper (Cung Xanh) when enemy frontlines stack armor and Striker's Flail when the lobby
rewards extra physical damage pressure.

Why these items: Jhin wants high attack damage and critical damage so his shots can remove priority
targets. Giant Slayer improves damage into high-health frontlines, while Last Whisper helps when
armor reduction is needed. Avoid giving Jhin purely defensive tank items unless no better holder
exists.

Vietnamese item aliases: Infinity Edge = Vô Cực Kiếm; Deathblade = Kiếm Tử Thần; Giant Slayer =
Diệt Khổng Lồ; Last Whisper = Cung Xanh.

Example answer in Vietnamese: Jhin nên lên Vô Cực Kiếm + Kiếm Tử Thần + Diệt Khổng Lồ. Nếu gặp
nhiều tanker giáp cao, thay bằng Cung Xanh; nếu cần thêm sát thương vật lý thì dùng Striker's
Flail.
