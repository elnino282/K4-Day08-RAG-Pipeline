# TFT augment and hero augment guide

This guide answers "lõi nào mạnh?", "lõi nào hợp Aatrox?", "lõi nâng cấp nên chọn sao?", and "augment nào hợp đội hình này?". Augments are patch-sensitive; cite patch context when giving meta advice.

## General augment priorities

| Augment | Tier / type | Use case | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advanced Loan | economy | strong when you can convert borrowed power into tempo or levels | high-risk if economy planning is weak |
| Aura Farming | combat / scaling | good when the board can use team-wide stats | flexible combat option |
| Cosmic Restart | comeback / combat | useful when fights are close and reset value matters | patch-sensitive |
| Epoch+ | scaling | good when the composition benefits from long-term scaling | listed among strong current-set augments by meta tier sources |
| Explosive Growth | economy / tempo | good when extra resources accelerate board strength | strongest when you can spend tempo cleanly |

## Comp-specific or hero-style augments

| Augment | Best with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Stellar Combo Aatrox | Aatrox reroll / N.O.V.A. setups | supports itemized Aatrox and low-cost reroll plans |
| Reach for the Stars | Stargazer boards | supports Stargazer-style scaling and reroll lines |
| Self Destruct | Gragas / frontline explosion comps | useful when the comp wants frontline burst or death-trigger pressure |
| Shieldmaiden | Leona / shield-heavy frontline boards | supports durable frontline and shield scaling |
| Bonk! | melee reroll / Leona-style boards | combat augment for low-cost reroll or melee pressure lines |
| Contract Killer | Pyke / assassin-style boards | useful when playing pickoff or rogue pressure comps |

Answering rule: for "Aatrox nên chọn lõi nào", recommend Stellar Combo Aatrox if the player is committing to Aatrox reroll, then mention generic combat/tank/economy augments if that specific hero augment is not offered. For "lõi nào mạnh nhất", say that the best augment depends on board state, stage, items, and comp; list current strong examples only with patch caveat.
