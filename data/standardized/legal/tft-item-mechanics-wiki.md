TFT Item Mechanics Wiki
Item | Teamfight Tactics
An item is a modular enhancement that grants bonuses and capabilities beyond what Champions and units have
access to by default. Items can be found from a variety of sources, including but not limited to: Encounters, Loot
Orbs, Augments, and Carousels or Realm of the Gods.
Items are commonly offered in the following types, with their estimated gold equivalents:
Component (~8g): Give a base stat.
Completed Item (~12g): Combine 2 Components for a Completed Item which give more stats and
usually an effect.
Radiant Item (~30g): A much stronger version of Completed Item.
Artifact (~18g): Give stats and a rare and powerful effect.
Support Item (~18g): Give a buff to the team and/or a debuff to the enemy team.
Spatula/Frying Pan: Combine with a Component for an Emblem which give a trait to the holder, or
combine with another Spatula/Frying Pan for a Tactician's Item which increase your team size by 1.
A champion can hold up to 3 items.

Item Holding
The player can equip champions with their items provided that:
It's a champion.
The player owns that champion (You can't equip your teammate's champions with your items in Double-
Up).
It's not a non-champion unit (such as Tibbers). Except to Tibbers in Lore & Legends.
It's not a Clone.
The champion has item spaces free.
Champions can be equipped with items before or during combat. To equip their champion with items, the player
can:
Clicking the item in the item bench, then clicking on top of the Champion.
Dragging the item from the item bench onto the Champion, then unclicking.
See below for interactions with dragging a Component onto a Champion holding a Component.
Some other conditions allow a champion to hold an item temporarily, such as (but not limited to):
Augments such as Binary Airdrop.
Traits such as Scrap.
Support Items such as Unstable Treasure Chest.

Item Crafting
Components can be combined with another component to craft it into one of three types:
Component + Component = Completed Item
Spatula/ Frying Pan + Component = Emblem
Spatula/ Frying Pan + Spatula/ Frying Pan = Tactician's Item as Tactician's Crown,
Tactician's Cape, and Tactician's Shield.
Crafting can be done before combat, or during combat by:
Right-click on a component in your item bench to pull up a list of its recipes. Click on a valid recipe,
then click Craft.
Drag a component onto another component in your item bench. Hold the component there for a few
seconds, then un-click to Craft. This can be cancelled by dragging it off the targeted component.
Drag a component onto a champion holding a component to combine into a Completed item.
Combining two copies of a champion who both hold a component will combine it into a Completed
item.
Ex: If you have two 1-star Garens each holding a Giant's Belt, getting a third Garen will
combine into a 2-star holding a Warmog's Armor.
Temporary components given mid-combat can not be crafted together.
If a champion has a Component that temporarily becomes a Completed Item during combat (for example
from Scrap), you can still drag another Component onto them as long as they have an open slot. This
will not create a Completed Item during combat; instead, the champion will simply hold both items. After
the fight ends and the temporary item turns back into its normal Component form, the two Components will
then automatically combine into a Completed Item.
Item Removing
Items can be removed from champions through various ways. If the item bench is full, it will instead drop as a
Loot Orb.
Selling a champion will drop their items held.
If you combine champions with a total of more than 3 items, the extra items will be dropped.
Crafting an Emblem on a champion with the same Trait will automatically drop off the champion.
Ex: A K.O. Coliseum Garen holding a Spatula will drop the resulting Emblem if it was crafted
into a Battle Academia Emblem because Garen already has the Battle Academia trait.
With an exception of Thief's Gloves, it will automatically drop out when you form it on a champion who
already holds an Item and a Sparring Gloves.
Using a Magnetic Remover or a Golden Item Remover.
After using a Reforger on a champion.
Some items, mainly those exclusively given by a Trait or Augment, can be removed by benching the
champion (moving the champion holding it from the board to the champion bench).
Some items that are only spawned from an existing board condition, such as Inkborn Fables's
Inkshadow's Inkshadow Items, will be removed (not dropped) from the champion once their condition
that created them is invalidated.
The extra items produced by Thief's Gloves / Rascal's Gloves / Radiant Thief's Gloves are tied
to the item and will not drop upon its removal.
Item Stacking
Most items stack additively with themselves.
Ex: 3 Crownguards each give shield, and each shield that expires gives the bonus AP.
The champion will start with 75% shield (25%x3).
As of the current edit, Crownguard gives 25% shield.
After one of the 25% shields is lost from damage, the Crownguard that is responsible will give its
bonus.
Durability has unique scaling that decreases as it approaches 100%.
The Attack Speed cap (5.00) can't be bypassed with items.
